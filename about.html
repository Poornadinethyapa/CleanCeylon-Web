About CleanCeylon

Empowering Sri Lankan communities through accessible waste management education and civic action.

Our Mission

CleanCeylon was developed to bridge the gap between household waste segregation and local recycling infrastructure. By providing a centralized directory for e-waste and recyclables, alongside community Shramadana tracking, we aim to reduce illegal dumping and promote a circular economy across the island.

The Platform

This interactive web application features a dynamic EcoFinder, verified collection center mapping, and an educational EcoChallenge module, built to foster sustainable environmental habits.

Academic Initiative
