Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about waste management in Sri Lanka.

CFL bulbs contain toxic mercury. Never throw them in general waste. Wrap them carefully in newspaper to prevent breakage and hand them over to a certified e-waste collection center like Cleantech.

Most local recycling centers in Sri Lanka do not accept standard polythene bags. It is best to use reusable cloth bags. If you must dispose of polythene, it goes into the non-recyclable general waste.

Gather local volunteers, coordinate with your Municipal Council or Pradeshiya Sabha for waste collection trucks, and list your event on our Community Cleanups board for visibility.
